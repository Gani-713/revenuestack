Case study structure

A clear story from business challenge to measurable impact.

Each case study follows the same decision-focused structure, so visitors can quickly understand the problem, the work, and what changed.

Objective

Define what the client needed to achieve and the commercial outcome that mattered.

Challenge

Show the constraint, inefficiency, or growth barrier limiting performance.

Strategy

Explain the recommendation, channel logic, and why that direction was chosen.

Execution

Summarize media, audiences, creative, tracking, testing, and optimization.

Results

Present verified outcomes with timeframe and a clearly defined metric.

Business impact

Connect the campaign result back to revenue, efficiency, quality, or scale.
